## Key concepts
- Ethical hacking — authorized attempt to find vulnerabilities before attackers do
- CIA triad — Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability
- Types of hackers — white hat, black hat, grey hat
- Attack phases — recon, scanning, gaining access, maintaining access, covering tracks

## Commands / tools
- `nmap -sV target` — service/version detection
- `whois domain.com` — domain registration lookup

## Interview-style Q&A
**Q: What's the difference between vulnerability assessment and penetration testing?**
A: VA finds and lists vulnerabilities; pentest actively exploits them to prove impact.

**Q: What are the 5 phases of hacking?**
A: Reconnaissance, scanning, gaining access, maintaining access, covering tracks.

## Certification-relevant points
- CEH exam loves phase-ordering questions — memorize the 5 phases in order
- Know the difference between active and passive reconnaissance
